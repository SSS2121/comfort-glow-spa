Privacy Policy and Data Treatment

At D3 Glow Spa, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy describes how we handle information when you visit our website.

1. Information Collection

Our website functions primarily as an informational catalog of our services. We do not directly collect personal data through forms, registrations, or internal databases on this site.

2. Usage Analytics

To improve your experience and understand how our site is used, we use Vercel Web Analytics. This service collects general site traffic information anonymously (such as page visits and device types) but does not track or store personally identifiable information (PII) or use intrusive third-party tracking cookies.

3. Booking and Contact

Our bookings and direct interactions are managed externally via WhatsApp. When contacting us through this medium, the information you choose to share (such as your name and booking details) will be used exclusively for scheduling and managing your appointment, under strict confidentiality standards.

4. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices. We recommend reviewing it periodically. Your continued use of the site following any changes constitutes acceptance of those changes.
